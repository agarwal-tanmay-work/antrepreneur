# Direction contract: Antrepreneur web app (home)

Development-only. Never copy into source, markup or bundles.

Mode: Persuade (first viewport) above Operate (colony, jobs, trust, simulation).

THESIS: The colony is visible. The whole page is one glass ant farm seen in cross-section, and the live colony lives in it: ants are real on-chain agents, chambers are their food stores, tunnels carry real work. Refuses the category default: a near-black crypto dashboard with neon KPI tiles and equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Sand ground with grain and strata that darken as you scroll (sand, deep sand, clay, loam, bedrock). Loam-brown tunnels and chambers. A molded toy-green plastic frame around the viewport, with the nav set into its top rail. Golden seeds are food. Ink is loam. Faces: Bricolage Grotesque (display), Schibsted Grotesk (text), JetBrains Mono only for numbers, hashes and keys.

STORY: A visitor understands that AI agents run their own little business, believes it because they see real ants answer real jobs and get paid by a contract that checks the judge, and then drops in a job or backs an ant.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The frame rail at the top (wordmark left, section links, network). Headline printed large on the sand at top left, three lines. One plain sentence and one line of live facts. A working "drop a job in" form under it. The colony cross-section fills the rest: a job chamber at the top, a tunnel down to every ant's chamber, seeds showing food, ants that have answered the open job standing in the job chamber.

FORM: Ant farm (formicarium) cross-section, first on the ordered list. seed: none (pinned) "Ant Farm" chosen by the user from three directions.

Signature interaction: ants walk the tunnels when their state changes on chain (answer a job, return home, the winner carries seeds back). Motion grammar: custom ease-out for UI, ease-in-out for travel, one hero entrance, nothing else enters on scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
